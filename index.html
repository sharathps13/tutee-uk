    Settle in the UK

    Your journey doesn't end at graduation.

    A Student visa doesn’t lead to permanent residence on its own. The pathway is degree, Graduate Route, a sponsored Skilled Worker visa and then Indefinite Leave to Remain. We guide you through each stage, and help you relocate with your family.

    Level up to settled 1 / 4 levels explored
    Achievement unlocked Settled in the UK Plan my route, free

    Stage 4 of 4

    Indefinite Leave to Remain

    Settle permanently after usually five continuous years on a work route such as Skilled Worker.

    The rules

    • You must usually have lived and worked in the UK for five continuous years on a qualifying route, such as Skilled Worker, in a job that keeps meeting the salary requirement.
    • Time on the Graduate Route does not count towards these five years.
    • You need to pass the Life in the UK test, meet the English language requirement (B1 level or above), follow all UK immigration rules and be of good character.
    • The government has consulted on a longer, “earned settlement” model. It is a proposal and not yet in the Immigration Rules; we check the latest rules for your case.

    How we help

    We track your qualifying years, prepare your ILR application and documents, and keep you updated on any rule changes.

    Next: After ILR you can usually apply for British citizenship.

    Stage 3 of 4

    Skilled Worker Visa

    Move into long-term, employer-sponsored work: the main qualifying route towards settlement.

    The rules

    • You need a formal job offer from a UK employer with a Home Office sponsor licence.
    • Since July 2025 the role must generally be at degree level (RQF level 6) or above.
    • You must meet the salary requirement for the job and the English language requirement; a UK degree usually covers the English.
    • You can switch from the Graduate Route without leaving the UK.

    How we help

    We help you target sponsor-licensed employers, check a job meets the rules and prepare your switch application.

    Next: Five continuous years on this route usually lead to Indefinite Leave to Remain.

    Stage 2 of 4

    Graduate Route

    Stay and work in the UK after your degree, with no employer sponsor needed: time to gain UK experience and build your network.

    The rules

    • Two years if you apply on or before 31 December 2026; 18 months if you apply on or after 1 January 2027.
    • Three years after a PhD or other doctoral qualification.
    • No sponsor and no job offer needed; you can work at any level or look for work.
    • It cannot be extended, but you can switch to another route such as Skilled Worker.

    How we help

    We plan your timing, apply before your Student visa ends and help you plan the move into a sponsored job.

    Next: Switch to a Skilled Worker visa when you find a qualifying job.

    Stage 1 of 4

    UK Student Visa

    Study at a UK university that holds a student sponsor licence.

    The rules

    • You need an offer and a CAS from a licensed student sponsor, plus proof of funds and English.
    • During term time you can usually work up to 20 hours a week on a degree-level course, in roles such as retail, hospitality, logistics, on-campus admin or tutoring. It helps with living costs, not tuition fees.
    • Dependants are only allowed on postgraduate research courses (such as a PhD or MRes) or government-sponsored courses.
    • When you finish your degree you can apply for the Graduate Route from inside the UK.

    How we help

    We choose courses that lead into the Graduate Route, prepare your CAS documents and run mock credibility interviews.

    Next: Complete your degree, then apply for the Graduate Route.

    Based on GOV.UK and the Immigration Rules, checked October 2026. Rules change; we confirm what applies to you at your free consultation.

    Relocating with your family?

    We help MRes and PhD students relocate to the UK with their families. As postgraduate research students, you can bring your dependants — your partner and children under 18 — on a Student visa.

    • MRes & PhD students: We confirm your course qualifies as postgraduate research, so your family can join you.

    • Dependant visas, handled: We prepare your partner's and children's applications, documents and interview prep.

    • Settle together: Move onto the Graduate Route and a Skilled Worker visa, where dependants have full work rights.
