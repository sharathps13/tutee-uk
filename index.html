    07Settle in the UK

    Your journey doesn't end at graduation.

    We guide you through the seamless pathways to build your career and relocate with your family. The UK offers structured pathways for international students to transition into the workforce and eventually settle permanently. With our expert guidance, navigating these steps becomes straightforward and stress-free.

    1. Step 1 of 4

      UK Student Visa

      Complete your study programme. We help you choose courses that lead straight into the Graduate Route.

    2. Step 2 of 4

      Graduate Route

      Stay and work in the UK for two years after graduation (three for PhD) with no sponsor required.

    3. Step 3 of 4

      Skilled Worker Visa

      Transition smoothly with our guidance to an employer-sponsored Skilled Worker visa for long-term work.

    4. Step 4 of 4

      Indefinite Leave to Remain

      Take the final step towards settlement — ILR after five continuous years on a qualifying work visa.

    Relocating with your family?

    We help MRes and PhD students relocate to the UK with their families. As postgraduate research students, you can bring your dependants — your partner and children under 18 — on a Student visa.

    • MRes & PhD students: We confirm your course qualifies as postgraduate research, so your family can join you.

    • Dependant visas, handled: We prepare your partner's and children's applications, documents and interview prep.

    • Settle together: Move onto the Graduate Route and a Skilled Worker visa, where dependants have full work rights.
